Clinical exposure

Learn closer to real clinical practice.

Mentorship can take different forms. The physician determines what is appropriate and available in each setting.

Observe clinical work

See how patient care, teamwork, and specialty-specific routines look in real clinical environments.

Discuss cases and reasoning

Ask questions, work through clinical thinking, and understand how experienced physicians approach decisions.

Interpret investigations

Explore how clinical findings and investigations are considered together in a real specialty context.

Understand the specialty

Get a closer look at the day-to-day reality of a field before making major career decisions.

Get career perspective

Have an honest conversation with someone who has direct experience in the specialty you are considering.

Reflect after the experience

Turn the experience into clearer questions, next steps, and a better sense of fit.
