What mentorship can look like

Share the parts of medicine you know best.

There is no single format. The experience can be shaped around your specialty, setting, and comfort level.

Clinic shadowing

Let trainees observe the realities of clinical workflow, communication, and specialty practice.

Case discussion

Share how you reason through cases and explain the questions that matter in your field.

Investigation interpretation

Show how investigations fit into clinical reasoning and specialty-specific decision making.

Specialty introduction

Give a realistic introduction to the work, rhythm, and questions that come with your specialty.

Career mentorship

Help trainees ask better questions about training, career choices, and what life in the specialty is like.

Professional guidance

Share practical lessons, professional perspective, and the experience that textbooks cannot fully capture.

You stay in control
