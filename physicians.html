What does being a mentor mean?

You decide what mentorship looks like.

Mentors choose what type of experience they are comfortable providing, when they are available, and how they prefer to interact with trainees.

Possible experiences

Clinic shadowing Clinical case discussion Specialty introduction OR observation where permitted Career mentorship Professional guidance

Mentorship activities are determined by the physician and must comply with institutional policies, professional regulations, and patient consent.
