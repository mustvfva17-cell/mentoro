Become a Mentor
Inside the experience

Before. During. After.

Clinical mentorship is more useful when expectations are clear before the session and reflection continues after it.

01 Before

Know what you're there to learn.

Start with a clear goal: a specialty you want to explore, a clinical question you want to understand, or a perspective you want to gain.

02 During

Observe. Discuss. Ask.

The experience may include clinical observation, case discussion, investigation interpretation, career guidance, or appropriate OR exposure.

03 After

Turn exposure into clarity.

Reflect on what you saw, what you learned, and whether the experience changed the questions you want to explore next.

Clinical boundaries matter. MENTORO facilitates mentorship; it does not replace formal medical education or the physician's professional responsibilities. Participation remains subject to consent, policy, and applicable rules.
