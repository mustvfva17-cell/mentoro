How it works

Two paths. One shared goal.

Whether you're requesting mentorship or offering it, the process is simple, transparent, and built around what you're comfortable with.

For Trainees

01

Create your profile

Tell us about your background, interests, and what you're hoping to learn.

02

Explore mentors

Browse specialists and consultants by specialty, city, and availability.

03

Choose an experience

Pick the type of mentorship that fits your goals — shadowing, discussion, or guidance.

04

Submit a request

Send a request with your availability and what you'd like to focus on.

05

Attend the mentorship experience

Meet your mentor and take part in the agreed clinical activity.

06

Learn and receive guidance

Ask questions, reflect on the experience, and plan your next step.

For Physicians

01

Apply as a mentor

Tell us about your specialty, experience, and how you'd like to contribute.

02

Create your professional profile

Showcase your background, interests, and the experiences you offer.

03

Choose your availability and experience type

Set the days, format, and activities you're comfortable providing.

04

Review suitable trainee requests

See requests from motivated students and junior doctors that match your profile.

05

Provide mentorship

Host the agreed session on your terms, in your own clinic or theatre.

06

Receive compensation for your time

Get compensated for the time, supervision, and mentorship you provide.
