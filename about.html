About us

Built by medical students who understand the gap.

We're medical students who experienced firsthand how difficult it can be to navigate medicine, discover specialties, find mentors, and gain meaningful clinical exposure — especially when you don't already have a professional network.

We believe curiosity and motivation should matter more than who you happen to know.

Mission

To make medical mentorship more accessible, structured, and meaningful.

Vision

Build a professional network where medical students and young physicians can learn directly from the people who have already walked the path.

Trust & ethics

Principles we won't compromise on.

Clinical mentorship only works if everyone involved — trainees, physicians, and patients — can trust how it's handled.

Patient privacy comes first

No patient information is shared outside appropriate clinical settings.

Mentorship complements, not replaces

Mentorship does not replace formal medical education.

Always within the rules

Clinical activities depend on institutional policies, physician discretion, applicable regulations, and patient consent.

No independent privileges

Trainees do not receive independent clinical privileges through the platform.

We connect — we don't control

The platform facilitates connections and organizes mentorship; it does not control clinical decisions.

Starting medical school?

Mentorship shouldn't start in the hospital. We're also developing peer tutoring and academic mentorship for younger medical students — from navigating first year and choosing resources to preparing for clinical years and exams.
