Mission

Make mentorship more accessible, structured, and meaningful.

We want motivated learners to be able to find guidance and real-world clinical perspective without relying only on who they already know.

Vision

A professional network where learners can learn directly from people who have walked the path.

We are starting in Egypt and building carefully around real clinical settings, real physicians, and real learner needs.

Trust & ethics

Clinical mentorship has to respect the people and settings around it.

Patients, physicians, and learners all deserve clear boundaries and responsible handling of clinical experiences.

Patient privacy comes first

Patient information should remain protected and handled only within appropriate clinical settings.

Mentorship complements medical education

mentoro is designed to add context and guidance, not replace formal education, rotations, or licensing requirements.

Every experience stays within the rules

Clinical activities depend on institutional policies, physician discretion, applicable regulations, and patient consent.

No independent clinical privileges

Trainees do not receive independent clinical privileges through mentoro.
