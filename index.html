The problem

Your medical career shouldn't depend on who you know.

Many students and junior doctors want to explore a specialty or learn from experienced physicians, but finding the right mentor often depends on personal connections, chance, or informal networks.

We're building a more accessible way to discover, connect, and learn from experienced physicians.

How it works

Three steps to real clinical exposure.

01

Choose

Explore specialties and find mentors aligned with your interests.

02

Connect

Request a mentorship experience that fits your goals and availability.

03

Learn

Gain real-world clinical exposure, ask questions, observe clinical practice, and learn from experience.

What you can experience

Mentorship shaped around real clinical practice.

Clinical Shadowing

Observe real clinical practice alongside experienced physicians.

Clinic Exposure

Understand how physicians approach patients, history taking, examination, diagnosis, and management.

OR Observation

Where permitted, observe appropriate surgical procedures and understand the workflow of the operating room.

Case Discussion

Discuss clinical reasoning and decision-making with experienced physicians.

Specialty Exploration

Experience a specialty before committing your career to it.

Career Mentorship

Ask questions about training pathways, residency, subspecialties, and life in the specialty.

Specific activities depend on the physician, institution, applicable regulations, and patient consent.
